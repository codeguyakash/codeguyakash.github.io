# 🔒 Privacy Policy

_Last updated: August 2025_

This Privacy Policy applies to all Chrome extensions developed and published by **Akash (codeguyakash)**, including but not limited to **Password Generator Extension**.  
We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring full transparency about how our extensions work.

---

## 📌 Data Collection & Usage

- **No Personal Data Collected**  
  Our extensions do not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information.

- **Local Functionality**  
  All core features (such as password generation, UI rendering, etc.) run entirely within your browser.  
  Nothing is sent to external servers unless explicitly stated in the extension description.

- **No Tracking**  
  We do not track browsing history, cookies, or user activity.

---

## 📌 Permissions

- Our extensions may request limited permissions (such as `scripting` or `storage`) strictly to enable functionality.
- Permissions are **never used** to access or transmit your private data.
- Any permission request is clearly explained in the Chrome Web Store listing.

---

## 📌 Third Parties

- We do not share any data with advertisers, analytics providers, or third-party services.
- Extensions are self-contained and function without external tracking scripts.

---

## 📌 Security

- Generated or processed data (like passwords in a password generator) remain on your device.
- Temporary data exists only during usage and is cleared when you close or reset the extension.
- For storing sensitive data like passwords, we recommend using a trusted password manager.

---

## 📌 Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements or compliance changes. Any updates will be published here and on the Chrome Web Store listing.
