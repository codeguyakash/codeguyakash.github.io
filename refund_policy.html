Cancellation and Refund Policy

This Cancellation and Refund Policy applies to all Chrome extensions developed and published by CODEGUYAKASH.

Nature of Products

All our products are digital Chrome extensions distributed through the Chrome Web Store or other authorized platforms. As such, once an extension is installed or downloaded, it cannot be returned or refunded due to the inherent nature of digital goods.

Refund Eligibility

Refunds are generally not provided after purchase or installation. However, exceptions may be considered in the following cases:

  • Accidental duplicate purchase made on the same account.
  • Technical issues preventing proper installation or activation of the extension.
  • Unauthorized transaction or billing error verified by our payment processor.

If your case qualifies under one of the above exceptions, please contact us with proof of purchase and relevant details.

Requesting a Refund

To request a refund (only under eligible cases), please contact us at:

  • to@codeguyakash.in

Include the following details:

  • Transaction ID or proof of purchase.
  • Name of the extension.
  • Description of the issue.

All refund requests will be reviewed within 5–7 business days.

No Guarantee of Refund

Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval. Each case is reviewed individually to ensure fair resolution.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to align with new payment regulations or platform requirements. The latest version will always be available on our official website and the Chrome Web Store listing.
