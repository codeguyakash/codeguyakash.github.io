Privacy Policy — Secure Pass Extension

This Privacy Policy applies to all Chrome extensions developed and published by CODEGUYAKASH, with particular reference to the Secure Pass extension. It governs the use of data across all current and future extensions published under this developer account.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring full transparency about how our extensions work.

Data Collection & Usage

  • No Personal Data Collected — None of our extensions collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information.
  • Local Functionality — All core features (such as password generation, UI rendering, or any other extension-specific functionality) run entirely within your browser. Nothing is sent to external servers unless explicitly mentioned in the extension's description.
  • No Tracking — We do not track browsing history, cookies, or user activity.

Permissions

  • Extensions may request limited permissions (such as scripting, storage, or host access) strictly to enable functionality.
  • Permissions are never misused to access or transmit private data.
  • Any permission request is clearly explained in the Chrome Web Store listing.

Third Parties

  • We do not share data with advertisers, analytics providers, or any third-party services.
  • Extensions are fully self-contained and do not include hidden trackers or analytics.

Security

  • Any generated or processed data (e.g., passwords, temporary user inputs, or local storage) remains on your device.
  • Data is cleared when you close, reset, or uninstall the extension.
  • For secure password storage, we recommend using a trusted password manager (our extensions are not designed for long-term storage).

Updates to this Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect new extensions, improvements, or compliance changes. The latest version will always be available on the Chrome Web Store listing and on our official website.
