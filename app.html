AutoAssist

AutoAssist is your smart missed call manager that automatically replies to calls via SMS when you are busy, driving, riding, or when your phone is in your pocket. Designed for safety and productivity, it evaluates system sensors to detect if you are traveling, automatically choosing the right template to keep callers informed while keeping your eyes on the road.

  • Auto-SMS Auto-Reply: Automatically sends templated SMS responses to missed calls.
  • Pocket Mode Detection: Uses proximity sensors to detect if the phone is face down or in a pocket to trigger silence or normal rules.
  • Travel/Motion Mode: Utilizes accelerometer sensor duty-cycling to identify when you are driving or riding, auto-replying with safety-oriented text templates.
  • Custom Templates: Create, edit, and delete customized auto-reply templates with bracketed tags (e.g. customized bot name).
  • Daily Limits & Duplicate Prevention: Safeguards against carrier costs by enforcing daily SMS limit caps and preventing duplicate replies to the same contact in a short time frame.
  • Status Dashboard: Track sent auto-replies, missed calls history, and carrier status logs right on the activity feed.
  • Privacy First: Processes all logs locally in a secure sandbox database. Your call logs and call data never leave your device.

Dev Launcher

Dev Launcher is a clean, distraction-free Android launcher built for developers, productivity users, and anyone who wants a pure, minimal home screen experience.

  • Minimal Home Screen: Show only your favorite apps to reduce distractions.
  • Full App Drawer: Fast alphabetical scroll with grid or list layout.
  • Monochrome App Icons: Black/light theme and white/dark theme support.
  • System Widget: Real-time clock widget with battery indicator.
  • Dynamic Themes: Full dark, light, and system theme support.
  • Optimized Performance: Haptic feedback toggle, R8-optimized for minimal battery and RAM usage.
  • Shortcuts & Actions: Quick shortcuts to Battery, Device Settings, and About Phone. Long-press any app to pin it, view info, or uninstall.
  • Privacy First: Zero permissions (no internet, no location, no contacts) and no user data collection.
  • Open & Lightweight: No bloat, no ads, no background services. Pure Android, Kotlin, and Compose.
